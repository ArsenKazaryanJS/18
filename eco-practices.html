Our Environmental Commitment

Sustainable manufacturing practices that protect our planet while delivering exceptional quality blinds and curtains.

Manufacturing With Purpose

Environmental responsibility is at the core of our manufacturing philosophy. We recognize that every business decision impacts our planet, and we are committed to minimizing our environmental footprint while maintaining the highest quality standards.

Our Norwich facility operates with sustainability as a guiding principle, from material selection through production processes to waste management and product lifecycle considerations.

Sustainable Material Sourcing

Responsible Supply Chain

We carefully select suppliers who share our environmental values. Our fabric suppliers must demonstrate sustainable practices including responsible forestry, reduced water usage, and minimal chemical processing.

Natural fibers play a significant role in our product range. Materials such as cotton, linen, and wool are renewable resources that biodegrade naturally at the end of their useful life.

  • Certified organic cotton options
  • Recycled polyester fabrics
  • Low-impact dye processes
  • FSC-certified wooden components
  • Recyclable aluminum and steel hardware
Learn About Our Materials

Material Certifications

We prioritize materials with recognized environmental certifications including OEKO-TEX Standard 100, which ensures fabrics are free from harmful substances.

Our commitment extends to packaging materials as well, using recycled cardboard and biodegradable protective materials wherever possible.

Energy-Efficient Production

Optimized Manufacturing

Our production processes are designed for maximum efficiency. Computerized cutting systems minimize fabric waste, while LED lighting throughout our facility reduces energy consumption significantly.

Waste Reduction

We implement comprehensive recycling programs for fabric offcuts, metal components, and packaging materials. Unusable textile waste is sent to specialist recycling facilities rather than landfills.

Water Conservation

Our manufacturing processes require minimal water usage. When water is necessary, we employ recycling systems to reduce consumption and ensure proper treatment before discharge.

Local Production

Manufacturing in the United Kingdom reduces transportation emissions associated with importing finished products. Our Norwich location allows us to serve customers efficiently while minimizing our carbon footprint.

Product Longevity and Maintenance

Built to Last

The most sustainable product is one that doesn't need replacing. Our blinds and curtains are manufactured with durability in mind, using quality materials and robust construction techniques.

We provide maintenance guidance to help customers extend the life of their window treatments, reducing the need for replacements and minimizing waste.

Repair and Refurbishment

Rather than discarding damaged products, we offer repair services for many components. Mechanisms can be serviced, fabrics can be replaced, and minor damage can often be remedied.

When products do reach the end of their useful life, we encourage customers to return items to us. We separate materials for appropriate recycling channels, ensuring minimal environmental impact.

Our products contribute to energy efficiency in buildings. Thermal linings in curtains reduce heat loss, while reflective blinds reduce heat gain, helping lower heating and cooling costs for our customers.
