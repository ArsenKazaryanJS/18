Product Guide

Discover our comprehensive range of blinds and curtains, all manufactured to the highest standards at our Norwich facility.

Understanding Your Window Treatment Options

Choosing the right window treatment depends on several factors including room function, light control needs, privacy requirements, and aesthetic preferences. Our product range offers versatile solutions for every space in your home or business.

Each product category serves specific purposes while maintaining our commitment to quality manufacturing and sustainable practices. Below you will find detailed information about our main product lines.

Roller Blinds Collection

Functionality Meets Style

Roller blinds offer a sleek, contemporary solution that works beautifully in any setting. The simple mechanism ensures smooth operation while the flat profile maximizes your view when raised.

Our roller blinds are available in three main fabric types:

  • Blackout fabrics for complete light blocking in bedrooms and media rooms
  • Dimout fabrics that filter light while maintaining privacy
  • Sheer fabrics for gentle light diffusion and daytime privacy

All roller blinds come with our premium control systems including chain operation, spring mechanisms, and motorized options for larger installations.

Vertical Blinds Range

Ideal Applications

  • Large picture windows
  • Patio and sliding doors
  • Office partitions
  • Commercial spaces
  • Conservatories
  • Bay windows

Versatile Light Control

Vertical blinds excel at covering wide windows and doorways. The individual louvres rotate to control light and privacy levels, or stack neatly to the side for an unobstructed view.

We manufacture vertical blinds in various materials including fabric, PVC, and aluminum, each offering distinct benefits. Fabric louvres provide softness and warmth, PVC offers durability and easy cleaning, while aluminum delivers a modern commercial aesthetic.

Our vertical blind systems feature smooth-gliding tracks and durable headrails designed for years of reliable operation.

Custom Curtains

Our curtain manufacturing service offers unlimited design possibilities. From traditional pinch pleats to contemporary eyelet styles, we create curtains that reflect your personal taste and complement your interior design.

Fabric Selection

Choose from our extensive fabric library featuring natural linens, luxurious velvets, practical cottons, and innovative blends. We source materials from reputable suppliers committed to sustainable production.

Lining Options

Standard, thermal, or blackout linings enhance functionality. Thermal linings improve insulation and reduce energy costs, while blackout linings ensure complete darkness for restful sleep.

Heading Styles

Select from pencil pleat, pinch pleat, eyelet, tab top, or wave headings. Each style creates a distinct look and works with different curtain pole or track systems.

Finishing Touches

Complete your curtains with coordinating tiebacks, decorative trims, or contrast borders. Our skilled seamstresses ensure every detail meets our exacting standards.
